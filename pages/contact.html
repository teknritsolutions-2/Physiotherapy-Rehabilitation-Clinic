Before you get in touch

Practical questions, answered

Can I ask which service is appropriate?+

Yes. Describe the kind of activity or concern you would like to discuss. A clinician assessment is needed to establish an individual care plan.

Can I send medical documents here?+

Please don’t enter medical records or sensitive health details in this form. Ask the clinic which secure channel to use.

Is my booking request confirmed immediately?+

No. A request is a preferred date and time, not an appointment confirmation. The clinic must confirm the details before you attend.
