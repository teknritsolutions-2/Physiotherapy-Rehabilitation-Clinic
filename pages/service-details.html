+ CARE / SERVICE GUIDE

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Build confidence in your next move.

+ A PERSONAL STARTING POINT

UNDERSTAND. PLAN.
MOVE FORWARD.

Movement assessment, strength and mobility work, and a guided return to the activities that matter to you.

Who may find this helpful?

People who want support discussing movement after an activity-related setback, or a structured conversation about returning to sport.

Your assessment

Discuss your goals and relevant history directly with the clinician. With your consent, they may look at your movement and explain appropriate options.

What to expect

Clear explanations, a plan discussed with you and opportunities to ask questions. Any home activities should come from your treating clinician.

Before you begin

Share any relevant clearance requirements with your clinician. This page is general information and does not establish suitability for treatment. Seek appropriate clinical help for new or urgent concerns.

+ GOOD QUESTIONS

A LITTLE CLARITY.
A BETTER START.

Do I need a referral?+

Referral requirements depend on your care and local rules. Ask the clinic before your first appointment; clinic contact details must be configured before launch.

What happens at the first assessment?+

You will discuss your concerns, activities and goals. Your clinician will explain the assessment, ask for consent and discuss appropriate next steps.

Is a booking request a confirmed appointment?+

No. This frontend saves a local request only. The clinic must confirm the appointment through a connected scheduling service.

What should I bring?+

Comfortable clothing, questions you would like to discuss and any relevant referral or clearance documents requested by your clinician. Do not upload medical documents to this local preview.
