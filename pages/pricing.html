+ GOOD QUESTIONS

A LITTLE CLARITY.
A BETTER START.

Do I need a referral?+

Referral requirements depend on your care and local rules. Ask the clinic before your first appointment; clinic contact details must be configured before launch.

What happens at the first assessment?+

You will discuss your concerns, activities and goals. Your clinician will explain the assessment, ask for consent and discuss appropriate next steps.

Is a booking request a confirmed appointment?+

No. This frontend saves a local request only. The clinic must confirm the appointment through a connected scheduling service.

What should I bring?+

Comfortable clothing, questions you would like to discuss and any relevant referral or clearance documents requested by your clinician. Do not upload medical documents to this local preview.
