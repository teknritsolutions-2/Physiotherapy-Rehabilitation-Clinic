Your questions, answered

Planning your care

What happens during an assessment?+

You’ll discuss your movement, everyday activities and goals. Your clinician will explain any assessment, ask for consent and talk through appropriate next steps.

Is my booking request confirmed immediately?+

No. A request is a preferred date and time, not an appointment confirmation. The clinic must confirm the details before you attend.

What should I bring?+

Comfortable clothing, your questions and any referral or clinical clearance documents requested by your clinician.
