The query and screenshots were recorded outside the repository during verification.

Before final commercial use, obtain jurisdiction-specific clearance covering identical and similar-sounding names, relevant classes/services, business names, common-law use and the proposed logo. The absence of an exact indexed or USPTO result does **not** establish availability or freedom to use the name in India or elsewhere.
