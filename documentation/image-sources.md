## Fonts and artwork

Self-hosted Plus Jakarta Sans and Source Sans 3 (400, 500, 600, 700) were downloaded through Google Fonts from fonts.gstatic.com. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License. Copies are included in `assets/fonts/`.

- https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/plusjakartasans/OFL.txt
- https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/sourcesans3/OFL.txt

Barlow Condensed and Public Sans files were removed. The Kinerelle logo restores the original angled movement symbol and circular accent. Its full and compact wordmarks are outlined vectors derived from the included Plus Jakarta Sans and Source Sans 3 fonts, whose licenses remain bundled. Flow lines and interface icons are original code-native SVGs. Google and Apple marks appear on the authentication provider buttons. Provider integration is not configured; the buttons show an unavailable message without simulating authentication.

The expanded ribbon system includes six original surface styles and matching static vector fallbacks (`care-ribbon*.svg`). These are generated from Kinerelle’s geometry and palette, without external artwork.

The current `care-ribbon*.svg` artwork was redrawn as original open curves with continuous teal/glacier/mint/pearl gradients. The previous ribbed surface and mesh texture were removed. These vector images are used directly in both animated and static views.

`care-ribbon-pricing.svg` and `care-ribbon-contact.svg` are original rounded-ended gradient forms sized to remain fully visible in their intro banners. Logo variants retain the original movement symbol and outlined Kinerelle name, with consistent optical alignment across light/dark and full/compact versions.

`care-ribbon-service.svg` is an original, fully contained rounded gradient arc for all service-guide variants.

The standalone sail, loop, Contact, Pricing and service-guide shapes are no longer rendered. Page intros, Home 1 and compact authentication backgrounds now reuse the original assessment-section `care-ribbon-arc.svg` as a full-width section-edge wave. The SVG allows proportional host stretching without internal letterboxing. Existing broad lower-section waves remain in place; no photographs changed in this update.
